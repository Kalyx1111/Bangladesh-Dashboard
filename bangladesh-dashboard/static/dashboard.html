Map basis: India's official survey depiction, as in the South Asia dashboards. Bangladesh is drawn in detail: its divisions (Natural Earth; this dataset does not show the Mymensingh division separately), its main rivers and towns, and the Rakhine and Chin states of Myanmar along the border. India is a plain outline with nothing plotted in it; Myanmar is plotted only near the border. The Bangladesh-Myanmar maritime boundary was settled by the ICJ in 2012, and the Bangladesh-India maritime boundary by the Permanent Court of Arbitration in 2014. Arunachal Pradesh is shown as India; China claims it as South Tibet. The Chittagong Hill Tracts and the Rakhine frontier are under contested control. The map shows no front lines and settles no dispute.

Darkweb & Threat Intel…
Sources: Ransomware.live (leak-site claims - group claims, not verified facts); Telegram threat-intel channels; and dark-web RSS sources reached through Tor that you verify yourself (data/onion_sources.json).
To read onion sources you need Tor on this PC: open Tor Browser (it listens on port 9150) or install and start the Tor service (port 9050). Keep it running, then press Refresh. Nothing is needed for the clear-web and Telegram sources. This dashboard never opens .onion pages in a browser and does not search or crawl the dark web.
